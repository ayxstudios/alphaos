# Calm pass, 2026-09-25/26 (lane/2026-09-26-calm)

Phone-first layout polish of the merged app (base 08e459b). Repair, never remove: every
feature and every piece of data is still in the page, some of it now folded behind a heading.

## How it was judged
- Local db `alphaos_calm26` (clone of the seeded tour db), app on :3477, mocks on.
- Added to that local db only, so the pages had something real to judge: 10 inbound messages
  (per business: one real customer reply on an order, one auto-reply, one Etsy "You made a
  sale" notice on an order, one unmatched person, one or two unmatched notices such as
  `mailer@shopify.com`, a newsletter, a mailer-daemon bounce) and realistic Etsy options on 3
  items (people/pets, size, "Would you like a rush order? (+$10): No Thanks", a background
  Yes, a long personalisation, an `_uploaded_image` URL).
- Headless Chromium at 390x844 (touch), logged in as tour-va, tour-admin and tour-designer,
  full scroll height captured; desktop re-checked at 1440x900 so nothing moved there.
- "Before" = the base files checked out in the same worktree, same data.

"Isla is waiting on a reply" at the top of Home and Today, and a bounce sat in Messages' Needs

opened every raw option (including an upload URL) and the whole history; the designer card
showed three raw shop questions with "No Thanks" answers.

## What changed
1. **Needs you ignores notifications and marketing** (`lib/email/noise.ts`, test
   `npm run test:email-noise`). Conservative: Etsy sale/shipped notices, no-reply, bounce,
   newsletter and other automated mailboxes, Etsy/Shopify and bulk-mail domains, auto-replies
   and delivery failures. A buyer's Etsy message, a customer on any normal address (info@ too)
   and a customer's "is the sale still on?" still count. Applied to the Today queue (Home's Do
   first, tiles and What is waiting read it), the unmatched counts (Home mail line, Today mail
   strip, Orders "emails need a reply"), and Messages, where notices move to their own folded
   **Notifications** section (still linkable, archivable, and in All mail). Ingest-time
   suppression is unchanged. Today's mail strip no longer calls failed sends "not matched".
2. **Home on a phone**: every chart card folds behind one line (title, a real summary such as
   "6 in progress across 4 designers", chevron). A tap opens it and the open state is
   remembered per chart (localStorage `home_fold_open`). The unmatched-mail line stays visible
   while folded. Lists (Do first, Due first) stay open. From md up the cards are as before
   (`components/home/fold-section.tsx`).
3. **Orders list on a phone**: a small card: number and stage chip, customer and due date
   (short date, rose when late), one quiet line with shop, designer and step clock, and the
   next action as a compact button. The whole card opens the order; the checkbox keeps 44 px.
   The customer line carries the existing `order:customer` tour anchor, so the search step has
   a visible sample on a phone.
4. **Order page on a phone** (`components/ui/phone-fold.tsx`): Activity (history) and Ask
   Alpha AI fold behind their heading; each item's raw options fold behind one summary line.
   Status, next step, customer facts, style, notes, photos, draft reply and messages stay open;
   Print and tracking (integration details) and Reassign were already disclosures. Folding is
   decided by CSS before hydration (no flash at either width); a link to `#notes` opens Activity
   on a phone, on load and on the same page. Desktop is unchanged.
5. **Designer card**: `lib/orders/options-summary.ts` turns shop options into one human line
   ("2 People + 1 Pet · 8×10 inches · Background colour · Please put the names…": no "No
   thanks", no links or `_` fields, a Yes becomes the thing said yes to). The board card shows
   that line (full list as the hover title); the opened card shows it with **All options**, one
   tap to every option in full, name over value.
6. **Messages on a phone**: not cramped, so layout kept; the link and archive fields get a
   44 px tap height, and the Notifications hint fits one line.
