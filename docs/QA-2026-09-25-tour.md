## What changed

- No ghost cursor, no demonstration, no "Watch how it works" anywhere (welcome card, "?" menu, /help).
- Each ringed thing: the page dims softly, the real element is cut out of the dim with a pigment ring
  (one pulse on arrival, then still), a thin curved arrow runs from the step card to it, and the card
  says what it is and what to press with "N of M". The step completes on the person's own click.
- To reach a step's page the tour rings the page's menu item first (sidebar; bottom tab; on a phone
  More, then the item). "Point me to it" on the Quick guide opens the page itself and lights the step.
