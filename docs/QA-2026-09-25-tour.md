- No ghost cursor, no demonstration, no "Watch how it works" anywhere (welcome card, "?" menu, /help).
- Each ringed thing: the page dims softly, the real element is cut out of the dim with a pigment ring
  (one pulse on arrival, then still), a thin curved arrow runs from the step card to it, and the card
  says what it is and what to press with "N of M". The step completes on the person's own click.
- To reach a step's page the tour rings the page's menu item first (sidebar; bottom tab; on a phone
  More, then the item). "Point me to it" on the Quick guide opens the page itself and lights the step.

Diagnosis (debug copy of the check, network and Postgres traced at the stall): the page's data had
fully arrived within 1s and the database was idle, yet the address did not change until the tour
itself moved on and sent its next save; a `router.refresh()` from outside released it the same way.

0a869ce's 45s wait sat BEFORE that save, so the tour and the page waited for each other (with the
older 15s wait the page arrived just after the next ring: run 5's stillness fail). Replacing
requestAnimationFrame made no difference (3 runs), so it is not the polling loop.

- 8dd5336: the next step sends its save first, then waits for the pressed page, then rings.
